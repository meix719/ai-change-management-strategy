
# AI Change Management Strategy for Google Workspace

## Overview
An individual academic proposal exploring how Google Workspace could adopt AI productivity metrics and support organizational change.

The plan connects a proposed KPI framework with stakeholder communication, employee training, and a phased implementation roadmap.

This is a coursework proposal, not an official Google initiative or client engagement.

## Business Question
How could Workspace teams measure the value of AI-assisted work and support adoption across engineering, product management, sales, and customer success?

## My Contribution
I developed the strategic analysis, proposed KPI framework, change management roadmap, training recommendations, communication plan, and presentation independently.

## Approach
- SWOT analysis
- Proposed strategic vision
- Current-state and future-state assessment
- KPI design
- Stakeholder and responsibility planning
- Training and communication planning
- Phased implementation roadmap

## Proposed KPI Framework
| Area | Proposed measure |
|---|---|
| AI adoption | Percentage of eligible users actively using AI features |
| Workflow automation | Share of defined workflows completed with AI assistance |
| Productivity | Change in task completion time, with output quality checks |
| Enterprise growth | Adoption of AI-enabled Workspace plans |
| Developer productivity | Change in development cycle time |
| Change readiness | Employee understanding and cross-functional alignment |

These are proposed measures. The project does not report observed implementation results.

## Change Management Plan
### Strategic Alignment
Define the productivity goals, KPI ownership, baseline measures, and executive sponsorship.

### Pilot and Integration
Test the framework with selected teams and workflows, provide role-specific training, and collect feedback.

### Scaling and Institutionalization
Refine the framework using pilot evidence and incorporate validated measures into regular reporting.

## Stakeholder Communication
- **Executives:** Strategic progress, investment assumptions, and business outcomes
- **Engineering:** Integration requirements, reliability, privacy, and testing
- **Product management:** KPI definitions, feature priorities, and roadmap alignment
- **Sales and customer success:** Customer value, product capabilities, and positioning
- **Employees:** Role changes, training resources, and feedback opportunities

## Recommendations
- Measure productivity alongside adoption and engagement.
- Establish baselines before setting final targets.
- Validate time savings without sacrificing output quality.
- Use pilots to test assumptions before broader rollout.
- Provide role-specific training and clear ownership.
- Maintain feedback channels throughout implementation.

## Limitations
- The analysis uses public information and academic assumptions, without access to Google's internal processes or KPI systems.
- Proposed adoption rates, productivity gains, and revenue targets are illustrative ambitions, not achieved results or validated forecasts.
- The presentation contains different targets and timelines across sections; these require reconciliation before implementation.
- Market statistics and product claims should be checked against dated primary sources before reuse.
- The revised vision is my proposal, not an official Google statement.
